06FAQ

Good questions

Can't find yours? Write to us on WhatsApp — we reply in English or Spanish.

Do I need an evaluation before my first treatment?+

Every first visit starts with a skin assessment so we can recommend what will actually work for you. If you already know what you want, we confirm it's right for your skin before starting.

Do you treat men?+

Yes. Our treatments are designed for women and men — from acne and texture to pigmentation and relaxing facials.

Is there any downtime?+

Recovery time depends on the treatment you receive. Before we begin, we explain what to expect and how to care for your skin afterwards.

How many sessions will I need?+

It depends on your skin and your goal. After your evaluation you'll receive a clear plan with the number of sessions and how often to come.

Where exactly are you?+

Inside The New Yorker Hotel, 481 8th Ave, at W 34th St — across from Penn Station.

Do you speak Spanish?+

Sí. Our whole team is bilingual — English and Spanish.
